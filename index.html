About Apex Guard

Detailing that comes to your driveway.

Apex Guard Detailing is a mobile detailing service built for Kent County drivers who want their vehicle protected and looking its best — without the hassle of dropping it off. We bring our full setup, water, and power to you, so you can get on with your day while we take care of the rest.

  • Fully mobile service We set up on-site at your home or workplace, anywhere in our Kent County service area.
  • Meticulous, hand-finished work Every panel, seam, and surface is carefully prepped, masked, and finished by hand.
  • Premium products only Professional-grade chemicals, coatings, and tools chosen to protect your vehicle’s finish.
  • Locally owned A Kent County-owned business you can trust with your vehicle, every time.
What We Offer

Detailing services

From a quick refresh to a full interior-and-exterior transformation, every package is tailored to your vehicle.

Exterior Detailing

Hand wash, wheel & tire clean, and a foam cannon spray wax for a quick, hydrophobic finish. (Paint correction & ceramic coating available as add-ons.)

Interior Detailing

Vacuum, spot treatment for cloth seats or leather seats cleaned, and a full clean of every surface and vent. (Steam cleaning & conditioning available with Ultimate Guard.)

Headlight Restoration

Restores cloudy, yellowed headlights to like-new clarity with a UV-protective sealant.

Engine Bay Cleaning

Degreased, cleaned, and dressed for a like-new look under the hood.

Paint Enhancement

Removes swirls and light scratches before sealant or coating — the first step toward showroom paint.
